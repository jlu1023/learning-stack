# Learner Model

How you hold each node, in your own words: the picture, framing, rule of thumb, chain or confusion your answers showed. `/learn:teach` and `/learn:quiz` append a line at the close of a session and read it back (through the engine's `show` and `due`) before they probe, teach or review that node, so a review after a long gap does not start cold. They do not mention it in chat unless you ask.

`- [date:: YYYY-MM-DD] [course:: CODE] [node:: Note Name] Held as: ... Shaky: ...`

- **From your own words** in that session only, never inferred from a bare right or wrong answer. At most one line per node per session, and none if nothing new showed.
- **The newest line is the current picture.** Old lines are never edited.
- **Format rules:** plain names, not links. Maths is LaTeX or Unicode.
